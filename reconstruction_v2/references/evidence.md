# GSX250R / GSX250R-A public evidence

Checked 2026-09-18. Public references are evidence for dimensions and part naming only; the owner photographs remain the primary appearance reference. These files are not calibrated blueprints.

## Confirmed specifications

The Haojue NEWGSX250R GSX250R-A specification page reports overall dimensions 2085 x 740 x 1110 mm, wheelbase 1430 mm, ground clearance 160 mm, seat height 790 mm, 15 L tank, front 290 mm disc and rear 240 mm disc, and tyres 110/80-17 M/C 57H front and 140/70-17 M/C 66H rear. It identifies the model as GSX250R-A. Source: https://en.haojue.com/NEWGSX250R/canshu.html (archived as `raw/haojue_NEWGSX250R_canshu.html`).

The Suzuki Japan parts catalogue is explicitly for GSX250RM1, GSX250RZM1, GSX250RAM1 and GSX250RZAM1. It is a parts reference, not a dimensional specification. Relevant figure/part evidence:

- Fuel tank, FIG.420A, applies to GSX250R/GSX250RA. Blue tank assembly `44100-20K00-W6B` is listed for body colour BY7; tank centre cover `44191-20K00` and fuel-tank supports/cushions are listed in the same figure (PDF p.57-58).
- Headlamp FIG.333A: headlamp assembly `35100-20K01` (PDF p.46-47).
- Side cowling FIG.485A applies to GSX250R/GSX250RA. Right/left side cowlings are `94475-20K00-YAY` and `94485-20K00-YAY` for the black YAY variant; the figure also lists the inner/under cowlings and `GSX`/`SUZUKI` emblems. Use only the shared family/geometry evidence when matching the blue owner motorcycle (PDF p.79).
- Seat FIG.505A: rider seat assembly `45100-20K01-QFE`, pillion seat assembly `45300-20K01-QFE` (black) (PDF p.85-86).

Source: https://www1.suzuki.co.jp/motor/support/parts_catalog_manage/files/GSX250RAM1_GSX250RAZM1.pdf (archived as `raw/suzuki_GSX250RAM1_GSX250RAZM1.pdf`).

## Variant boundary

Use GSX250R-A / GSX250RA family evidence. Do not use GSX250R-F evidence: it is a different variant designation and is not the user-selected Chinese GSX250R-A. The catalogue's `GSX250RZ/GSX250RZA` figures are colour/variant alternatives; they are retained only where the figure explicitly shares geometry, and their colour parts are not asserted for the subject motorcycle.

## Not determined by these sources

The sources do not establish photo-camera calibration, hidden mounting points, exact fairing surface curvature, guard-bar dimensions, phone mount/damper arrangement, Chinese plate dimensions/characters, or exact decal outlines. The catalogue diagrams and web product page must not be treated as scan data or a 100% external appearance guarantee. Brake rotor diameters and tyre sizes are confirmed specifications, while rim, tyre sidewall, decal and paint appearance remain photograph-led.
